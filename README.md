### 🛡️ What I do

I help organisations adopt AI **securely, at scale**.

AI agents now act on live systems, hold credentials and make decisions. My work sits where that creates both value and risk: designing the security architecture and governance that let teams move fast with AI without losing control.

- 🤖 **Agentic AI:** building AI agents and multi-agent systems, and taking AI platforms from prototype to production
- 🔐 **AI security:** secure onboarding of AI agents, **A2A** and **MCP** connections with OAuth, least-privilege access and clear ownership
- 🧰 **Background:** cybersecurity and privileged access management (CyberArk), so every identity gets exactly the access it needs, AI agents included
- ⚡ **How I build:** hands-on, with multi-agent development workflows in **Claude Code**
